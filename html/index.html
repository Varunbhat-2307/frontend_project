Living well, together

A few things
we agree on.

Good shared living starts with a little consideration. Here are the basics that help everyone feel at home.

Questions? Get in touch →
01Look out for each other

Respect your neighbors, keep shared areas welcoming, and let the team know if something needs attention.

02Keep the quiet hours

Please keep noise low between 10 pm and 7 am so everyone can get the rest they need.

03Make guests feel welcome

Visitors are welcome when signed in. Residents are responsible for their guests and should check current visitor hours with reception.

04Leave shared spaces ready

Clean up after yourself in the kitchen, dining room, laundry, and study areas. A little effort goes a long way.
